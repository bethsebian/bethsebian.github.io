About

I've spent over ten years as an engineer and manager in software companies, focusing on products that serve real people. Earlier in my career, after earning my degree from Harvard Divinity School, I worked in the policy and nonprofit space on projects advancing civic engagement, restorative justice, and local reform.

I believe human thriving depends on restoring our relationship with the land and with each other. I suspect we can enlist modern technology in this project, but doing so will require reorienting our mindsets and incentive structures toward dignity, interdependence, and accountability.

Connect
